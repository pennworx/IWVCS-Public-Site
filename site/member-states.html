Before you cross state lines

Where the Compact applies.

Forty-nine states participate in the Interstate Wildlife Violator Compact. If you have a suspension, contact the wildlife agency in every state where you plan to hunt, fish, or trap.

Official agency contacts

Open the agency for your state.

Choose a state to visit its official fish and wildlife agency website for licensing, eligibility, suspension, and contact information. Only the destination state can confirm whether you may buy a license or participate there.

Why the list alone is not enough

Your eligibility can differ by state.

Participation does not mean every state treats every violation or suspension identically. Each state applies its own law and provides its own notice and appeal process.
